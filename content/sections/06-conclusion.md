## Conclusion

We evaluated Astra’s zero-shot control of a mobile manipulator on diverse household tasks, without robot-specific training, demonstrations, or hand-written skills. We show that an off-the-shelf general-purpose vision-language model can translate household instructions into multi-step plans, coordinated movements, and adaptive behavior. We also identify key limitations in fine-grained, contact-rich manipulation, reasoning about dependencies between steps, collision avoidance, and efficiency. Our ablations further reveal how common system design choices affect performance. We hope these findings provide useful insights for the community and help advance the development of general-purpose robotic systems.
